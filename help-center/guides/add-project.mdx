## Overview

Adding a new project organizes your docs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dashboard">
    Sign in and go to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Project'">
    Find and select the "New Project" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Project Name">
    Type a name that identifies your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & Start Adding Docs">
    Click "Save" and begin creating documents.
  </Step>
</Steps>
