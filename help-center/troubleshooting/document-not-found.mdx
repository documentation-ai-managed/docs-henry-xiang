## Symptoms

- Error: "Document not found"
- Page fails to load

## Causes

- Document was deleted
- Incorrect link or typo

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Document URL">
    Double-check the URL for typos or incorrect names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore from Trash">
    Look in Project > Trash. Restore if found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recreate Document">
    If missing, use "New Document" to recreate.
  </Step>
</Steps>
